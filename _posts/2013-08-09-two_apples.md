One day they asked the great Orhan-fu-tzu: "What is the difference between an apple and a thought?"


Orhan-fu-tzu was silent for a moment, then he answered:
"If two persons each have one apple and swap them, then both will still have one apple. But if two persons each have a thought and exchange them, then both will have two thoughts. And that is the difference."
