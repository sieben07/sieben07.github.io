One day, they asked the great Orhan-fu-tzu:
"What is the difference between an apple and a thought?"

Orhan-fu-tzu was silent for a moment. Then he answered:
"If two people each have one apple and they exchange them, each still has one apple.
But if two people each have one thought and they share them, then both will have two thoughts.
And that is the difference."
