One day they asked the great Orhan-fu-tzu:"What is the difference
between a apple and a thought?" Orhan-fu-tzu was silent for a
moment, then he answered:" If two persons have one apple each and
swap them with each other, then both again will have one apple. If
two persons have a thought and exchange those with each other both
will than have two thoughts. And that is the difference."
